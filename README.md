# FocusTodo (Todo List - Pro)

FocusTodo is a professional, feature-rich desktop task management application designed to maximize your productivity using the Eisenhower Matrix, integrated time-tracking, and smart daily accountability features. 

Ready to use out of the box—no installation required!

## ✨ Features

- **Eisenhower Matrix Task Management**: Categorize your tasks into four quadrants to prioritize effectively:
  - Q1: Urgent & Important 🔥
  - Q2: Not Urgent, Important 🚀
  - Q3: Urgent, Not Important 🗣️
  - Q4: Not Urgent, Not Important 🗑️
- **Integrated Stopwatch & Time Tracking**: Track exactly how much time you spend working. Includes all-time stats and daily tracking.
- **Floating Mini-Timer (OLED Friendly)**: Switch to a minimal, draggable, and OLED-friendly floating timer widget to keep track of time without taking up screen space.
- **Smart Automation**: 
  - **Daily Reset**: Automatically clears completed tasks at the end of your customized work day.
  - **Auto-Demotion**: Tasks lingering in Q1 (Urgent & Important) for more than 48 hours are automatically demoted to Q3 to keep your priorities realistic.
- **Statistics & Streaks**: Visualize your productivity with built-in stats and daily streaks.
- **Global Hotkeys**: Control the timer (default: `Alt+x`) and toggle mini-mode (default: `Alt+z`) from anywhere on your PC, even when the app is in the background.
- **Robust Crash Recovery**: Never lose your timer progress if your system closes unexpectedly.
- **Highly Customizable**: Adjust window sizes, daily schedules, colors, sounds, and more directly in the app settings.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Getting started is completely plug-and-play:

1. Download the latest `FocusTodo.exe` from the Releases page.
2. Place the executable in a folder of your choice.
3. **Double-click `FocusTodo.exe` to run!** 

*(Note: The app will automatically create a `data.json` file in the same folder to securely save your tasks and settings locally.)*
